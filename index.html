Privacy Policy for Auto Care Pro

Effective date: 26 April 2026

Auto Care Pro is a vehicle utility app that helps users manage vehicle information, reminders, and nearby fuel station discovery. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled.

1. Information the app may process

  • Location data: The app may access your device location when you use nearby fuel station features.
  • Vehicle and reminder information: Information such as vehicle details and reminder data that you enter into the app.
  • Diagnostic and technical information: Limited technical information may be processed by the app or operating system for functionality, troubleshooting, and network communication.

2. How location is used

Location is used to find nearby fuel stations and to improve the relevance of nearby station results. Location is used only for app functionality.

3. How your information is stored

Vehicle and reminder information is primarily stored on your device. The app may communicate with third-party services or your chosen backend services to retrieve fuel station and fuel pricing information.

4. Third-party services

The app may request data from third-party or external services such as:

  • mapping or navigation services such as Google Maps when you choose to open a station in navigation
  • fuel price and station data providers
  • infrastructure services used by the developer to support app functionality

5. Purchases

If the app offers subscriptions or one-time premium purchases, payments are processed by Google Play and not directly by the app developer.

6. Data sharing

The developer does not sell your personal data. Data is only used as needed to provide core app functionality, improve reliability, and deliver requested features.

7. Data retention

Data that you store locally in the app remains on your device unless you remove it, uninstall the app, or a future sync feature is explicitly enabled by you.

8. Your choices

  • You can deny location permission, but nearby fuel features may not work correctly.
  • You can remove stored vehicle or reminder data from within the app where supported.
  • You can uninstall the app at any time.

9. Children

Auto Care Pro is not directed to children.

10. Security

Reasonable steps are taken to protect app functionality and user information. However, no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Updated versions will be posted at the policy URL.

12. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact the developer using the support email listed on the Google Play store listing.
